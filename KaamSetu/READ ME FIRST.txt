HOW TO OPEN

Quickest: double-click "Open KaamSetu.html". It works on its own, even straight from inside the zip.

Or:
1. Right-click this zip and choose "Extract All".
2. Open the KaamSetu folder.
3. Double-click "Open KaamSetu.bat".

The website opens in your browser. Sign-up and bookings save to Supabase.
If sign-up says "check your email", turn OFF "Confirm email" in Supabase: Authentication > Sign In / Providers > Email.
